# Anime visual styles

Default: Cinematic Japanese 2D Anime — clean controlled linework, coherent cel shading, expressive faces, painted backgrounds, motivated light, and restrained secondary motion.

| Style | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Hand-Drawn Anime | Organic contours, deliberate drawings, subtle texture, stable silhouettes |
| Modern Anime Film | Detailed painted environments, controlled bloom, layered light, nuanced expressions |
| Fantasy Anime | Clear costume silhouettes, luminous magic with a consistent color rule, readable world scale |
| Dark Fantasy Anime | Muted palette, directional shadow, selective highlights, legible subjects |
| Watercolor Anime | Paper texture, soft pigment edges, restrained washes, stable face details |
| Detailed Anime Illustration | Fine linework and ornament; limit motion complexity to protect detail |

Choose one primary style and a compatible lighting/palette system. Define key light direction, contrast, dominant colors, weather, atmospheric density, foreground/background separation, and animation treatment. Preserve reference-specific styles when requested. Avoid contradictory photorealistic skin and flat cel shading unless explicitly separating characters or elements. Do not add weather or particles that obscure key actions. Match color and light across cuts unless a motivated transition explains the change.

These are aesthetic instructions, not official model presets. General video projects may use the user's requested non-anime style with the same timing and continuity discipline.
