# Final quality gate

Review and repair before delivery:

- Timeline starts at 0; positive scene durations sum to the requested total; boundaries meet with no gaps/overlap. Transition accounting is explicit.
- Scene count and input mode match the request; unsupported long clips have an assembly plan.
- Every reference has a clear role; no fabricated inspection, unbound tags, or invented unseen identity details.
- Face, hair, outfit, accessories, proportions, and style stay locked except authorized changes.
- Dialogue is exact, attributed, within scene bounds, and naturally speakable with pauses; timing estimates are labeled outside the prompt when needed.
- Camera movement is achievable within the shot, focus targets exist, and simultaneous directions do not conflict.
- Motion has causal order and sufficient time; hands, props, screen direction, contact, and momentum are consistent.
- Weather, location, light, emotion, and transition states connect across cuts.
- Sound aligns with events; voice priority and intentional silence are clear.
- Story motivation and emotional change are visible; remove excess events when duration is tight.
- Prompt contains all twelve production elements, concise actionable language, no user-facing explanation, and no unfilled placeholders.
- Output includes Thai A/B sections, one final code block C, and D only when useful.
- Platform claims have sources; techniques and experiments are distinguished. No invented API parameters, guaranteed results, or unverified resolution claims.

For an actual rendered video, inspect frame transitions, identity drift, hands, outfit, lip sync, and audio timing before claiming success. Report observed defects and refine only where supported. Prompt review alone cannot validate rendered behavior.
