## C. FINAL SEEDANCE PROMPT (English + Thai Dialogue)

```text
VIDEO CONFIGURATION
Text-to-video; 12-second editorial sequence; 3 scenes; desired 16:9 composition.
REFERENCE ASSET MAPPING
No reference assets; original fictional adult swordswoman.
CHARACTER IDENTITY LOCK
Adult swordswoman: angular face, amber eyes, tied-back dark hair, burgundy cloak, charcoal tunic, leather boots, sheathed sword at left hip. No costume or weapon changes.
STORY OVERVIEW
From a dangerous obstacle to controlled determination.
TIMESTAMPED SCENE BREAKDOWN
Scene 1, 0–3 s: A swordswoman runs left to right toward a narrow broken bridge gap. Hard cut at the endpoint.
Scene 2, 3–8 s: She pushes off the intact edge, clears the gap, and lands on the opposite intact stone ledge. Hard cut at the endpoint.
Scene 3, 8–12 s: She regains balance and looks toward a distant gate on screen right. End on the final state.
CAMERA DIRECTIONS
Scene 1: Wide side view, short lateral track.
Scene 2: Medium-wide side tracking shot; preserve left-to-right travel.
Scene 3: Medium locked low-angle shot.
CHARACTER ACTIONS
Scene 1: She keeps her sheathed sword secured at her left hip and lowers her center of gravity.
Scene 2: Show push-off, airborne arc, then bent-knee landing in that order; cloak trails behind her.
Scene 3: Dust settles after landing; her right hand opens to regain balance, then relaxes.
THAI DIALOGUE
No dialogue or narration.
AUDIO DIRECTIONS
Wind under all shots. Footfalls in scene 1; landing thud around 7 s; grit settles in scene 3. A restrained percussion pulse rises during the leap.
VISUAL STYLE
Cinematic Japanese 2D Anime; clean contours, stable cel shading, painted backgrounds, soft overcast directional light, muted blue shadows with warm skin accents. Keep faces legible.
CONTINUITY RULES
Carry each scene's final pose and prop state into the next shot. Preserve outfit, hair, eye color, setting, light direction, and screen direction. Keep hand occupancy and object contact physically coherent. Use only motivated movements.
QUALITY CONSTRAINTS
Stable facial identity and anatomy; readable hands when visible; no costume swaps, extra props, unintended text, or unexplained camera cuts. Keep motion restrained enough for the allocated time.
```
